==============================================================================
SECTION 1 — GENERAL WORKPLACE SAFETY FRAMEWORK
==============================================================================
PURPOSE:
This document establishes the general workplace safety standards for all
SmartFactory production, warehouse, and support areas. It covers hazard
identification, emergency procedures, zone safety rules, and the responsibilities
of all personnel on site.

==============================================================================
SECTION 2 — ZONE DEFINITIONS AND SAFETY CLASSIFICATIONS
==============================================================================
Zone A — Assembly Line Area:
  Risk Level: HIGH
  Primary hazards: Moving machinery, pinch points, repetitive motion,
                   electrical equipment, overhead cranes.
  Mandatory PPE: Hard hat, safety vest, closed-toe safety footwear,
                 safety glasses in designated areas.

Zone B — Loading Dock / Logistics Area:
  Risk Level: HIGH
  Primary hazards: Forklift traffic, vehicle movements, loading/unloading
                   operations, edge fall risk at dock levellers.
  Mandatory PPE: Hard hat, high-visibility vest, safety footwear.
  Additional rules: No pedestrian access during active vehicle movements without
                    a spotter. All forklift operators must hold a valid permit.

Zone C — Warehouse / Storage Area:
  Risk Level: MEDIUM-HIGH
  Primary hazards: Racking collapse risk, falling stored materials, forklift
                   traffic, narrow aisles.
  Mandatory PPE: Hard hat, safety vest, safety footwear.

Zone D — Packing Area:
  Risk Level: MEDIUM
  Primary hazards: Ergonomic risk (repetitive tasks), packaging machinery,
                   cutting tools.
  Mandatory PPE: Safety gloves, closed-toe footwear. Hard hat required only
                 near conveyor systems or overhead structures.

==============================================================================
SECTION 3 — GENERAL SAFETY RULES (ALL ZONES)
==============================================================================
  1. Never operate machinery or equipment without authorisation and training.
  2. Keep walkways and emergency exits clear at all times.
  3. Report any hazard, near-miss, or incident immediately to your supervisor.
  4. Do not use mobile phones or personal audio devices while operating machinery.
  5. All spills must be cleaned up immediately using appropriate materials.
  6. No food or drink on the production floor.
  7. Horseplay and running on the factory floor are strictly prohibited.
  8. All visitors must be escorted by an authorised employee and must wear
     the full PPE kit issued at reception.
  9. Fatigue: workers who feel unfit for duty must notify their supervisor.
     Do not operate heavy machinery when fatigued.
 10. Lone working in high-risk zones is prohibited without a check-in protocol.

SECTION 4 — HAZARD IDENTIFICATION AND REPORTING
==============================================================================
4.1 Hazard Categories:
  - Immediate danger (risk of injury NOW): Stop all work, alert nearby workers,
    notify supervisor immediately.
  - Near-miss (potential injury, no injury occurred): Report to supervisor within
    1 hour. Complete a near-miss report form.
  - Unsafe condition (deteriorating equipment, damaged floor, inadequate
    lighting): Report to maintenance via the facility management system within
    the same shift.

4.2 Reporting Process:
  1. Identify and communicate the hazard verbally to nearby workers.
  2. Where safe to do so, isolate the hazard (barrier, safety cone, lockout).
  3. Report using the facility incident management system (or paper form if
     the system is unavailable).
  4. Do not leave an uncontrolled hazard without notifying someone.

==============================================================================
SECTION 5 — EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
==============================================================================
5.1 Fire:
  - On discovering a fire: activate the nearest fire alarm pull point.
  - Call emergency services.
  - Evacuate all personnel via the designated evacuation routes.
  - Do NOT use lifts during evacuation.
  - Muster at the designated assembly point and await a headcount.
  - Do NOT re-enter the building until the fire warden or emergency services
    confirm it is safe.

5.2 Medical Emergency:
  - Call for the nearest trained first aider.
  - Contact emergency services if the injury is serious.
  - Do not move an injured person unless they are in immediate danger.
  - Preserve the scene for incident investigation (without compromising care).

5.3 Chemical Spill:
  - Alert all workers in the immediate area.
  - Refer to the Safety Data Sheet (SDS) for the material involved.
  - Use the spill kit appropriate for the chemical type.
  - Ventilate the area if safe to do so.
  - Inform the EHS team immediately.

5.4 Power Failure:
  - Emergency lighting will activate automatically.
  - Isolate all powered equipment before attempting to exit machinery.
  - Follow supervisor instructions for evacuation or shelter-in-place.

SECTION 6 — LOCKOUT / TAGOUT (LOTO) PROCEDURE
==============================================================================
Before performing any maintenance, cleaning, or adjustment on machinery:
  1. Identify all energy sources (electrical, pneumatic, hydraulic, gravitational).
  2. Notify all affected workers.
  3. Shut down the equipment using the normal stopping procedure.
  4. Isolate each energy source using the designated isolation point.
  5. Apply a personal lockout padlock and tag to each isolation point.
  6. Release or restrain any stored energy (bleed pneumatics, block elevated parts).
  7. Verify isolation by attempting to start the machine (zero energy verification).
  8. Perform the maintenance task.
  9. Remove all tools and materials.
 10. Remove locks and tags only by the person who applied them.
 11. Notify workers that the equipment is being returned to service.
 12. Re-energise the equipment.

NEVER remove another worker's lockout device. If a worker has left site with a
lock in place, follow the site's emergency lock removal procedure with supervisor
and EHS manager authorisation.

SECTION 7 — CCTV SAFETY MONITORING INTEGRATION
==============================================================================
SmartFactory facilities use AI-powered CCTV monitoring to detect:
  - Workers without mandatory PPE (helmets, vests, masks, goggles).
  - Unauthorised access to restricted zones.
  - Unsafe worker behaviours in high-risk areas.

When an AI alert is raised:
  1. The monitoring system logs the incident automatically with timestamp,
     camera location, zone, and violation type.
  2. The duty supervisor receives a notification in the SmartFactory Copilot
     dashboard.
  3. The supervisor investigates within 5 minutes of alert generation.
  4. Corrective action is taken and the incident is closed in the system.
  5. Persistent violations trigger escalation to the EHS manager.

Workers are informed that CCTV monitoring is active in all production zones
for their safety and the safety of their colleagues.

SECTION 8 — INCIDENT INVESTIGATION PROCESS
==============================================================================
All incidents (injuries, near-misses, property damage, environmental releases)
must be investigated to identify root causes and prevent recurrence.

Investigation levels:
  - Level 1 (supervisor): Near-misses and minor first-aid injuries.
    Timeline: Completed within 24 hours.
  - Level 2 (EHS + department manager): Lost-time injuries, significant
    property damage. Timeline: Completed within 72 hours.
  - Level 3 (senior management + EHS): Serious injuries, dangerous occurrences,
    regulatory reportable events. Timeline: Immediate notification to management;
    full report within 7 days.

Root Cause Analysis methods used:
  - 5-Whys for routine incidents.
  - Fishbone (Ishikawa) for complex multi-factor incidents.
