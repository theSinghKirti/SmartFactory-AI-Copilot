==============================================================================
SECTION 1 — HARD HAT / SAFETY HELMET STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
==============================================================================
PURPOSE:
This procedure establishes the requirements for the selection, use, inspection,
and replacement of safety helmets (hard hats) in SmartFactory facilities to
protect workers from head injuries caused by falling objects, collisions with
fixed structures, and electrical hazards.

==============================================================================
SECTION 2 — APPLICABLE ZONES
==============================================================================
Safety helmets are MANDATORY in the following areas:
  - All assembly lines and production floors.
  - Loading docks and vehicle operating areas.
  - Warehouse storage and racking areas.
  - Maintenance workshops and engineering bays.
  - Any area with overhead structures, cranes, or lifting equipment.

Safety helmets are RECOMMENDED (but may be discretionary) in:
  - Enclosed office areas within production buildings.
  - Visitor walkways with physical barriers separating them from operations.

==============================================================================
SECTION 3 — HELMET SELECTION CRITERIA
==============================================================================
3.1 Class Ratings:
  - Class G (General): Protection against impact and penetration.
    Voltage protection up to 2,200 V. Suitable for most production areas.
  - Class E (Electrical): Protection against impact and penetration.
    Voltage protection up to 20,000 V. Required in electrical hazard zones.
  - Class C (Conductive): Impact protection only. NOT suitable for electrical
    work. Suitable for areas with no electrical hazard.

3.2 Suspension Systems:
  - 4-point or 6-point suspension recommended for all industrial use.
  - Ratchet or pin-lock adjustment systems preferred for comfort and fit.

3.3 Colour Coding (recommended):
  - White: Supervisors and managers.
  - Yellow: General workers.
  - Blue: Maintenance and engineering.
  - Red: Fire wardens and first aid responders.
  - Orange: Visitors and contractors.

==============================================================================
SECTION 4 — PRE-USE INSPECTION CHECKLIST
==============================================================================
Before every shift, workers must visually inspect their helmet for:
  [ ] Cracks, dents, or punctures in the shell.
  [ ] Fraying, tears, or broken components in the suspension webbing.
  [ ] Discolouration, chalking, or brittleness (signs of UV degradation).
  [ ] Loose or missing components (suspension clips, chin strap).
  [ ] Evidence of chemical exposure or paint contact.

If any defect is identified:
  - Remove the helmet from service immediately.
  - Tag it as "DEFECTIVE — DO NOT USE".
  - Obtain a replacement from the PPE station before entering any work area.

==============================================================================
SECTION 5 — CORRECT WEARING PROCEDURE
==============================================================================
  1. Position the helmet squarely on the head.
  2. Adjust the suspension for a snug but comfortable fit.
  3. The helmet brim must face forward unless the task specifically requires
     a reversed orientation (permitted only for certain close-clearance tasks
     — check with your supervisor).
  4. Chin straps must be fastened when working at height or in high-wind areas.
  5. No materials (stickers not covering inspection marks, materials inside the
     shell) should be placed between the helmet and the head.

PROHIBITED MODIFICATIONS:
  - Painting the shell (damages integrity and conceals defects).
  - Drilling ventilation holes.
  - Storing items inside the helmet shell between the shell and suspension.

==============================================================================
SECTION 6 — REPLACEMENT SCHEDULE
==============================================================================
Helmet shell: Replace every 5 years from the date of manufacture
              (stamped inside the shell) or immediately after any significant
              impact, regardless of visible damage.

Suspension system: Replace every 12 months or when any wear or damage
                   is detected.

Mandatory immediate replacement triggers:
  - Any head impact during an incident (even without visible damage).
  - Exposure to chemicals or extreme heat.
  - Visible cracking, crazing, or brittleness.

==============================================================================
SECTION 7 — CCTV DETECTION AND RESPONSE
==============================================================================
When the CCTV safety monitoring system detects a worker without a helmet:
  1. An automatic alert is raised in the SmartFactory Copilot incident log.
  2. The area supervisor receives a notification within 60 seconds.
  3. The supervisor must respond by:
     a. Identifying the worker using the CCTV frame reference.
     b. Approaching the worker directly.
     c. Directing the worker to a PPE station to collect a helmet.
     d. Ensuring the helmet is correctly fitted before work resumes.
  4. The supervisor closes the incident in the system with the corrective
     action taken.
  5. If the worker cannot be identified from the CCTV feed, a zone-wide
     announcement must be made to remind all workers in that area.

==============================================================================
SECTION 8 — REPEATED VIOLATIONS — ESCALATION PATH
==============================================================================
  1st violation: Verbal correction. Logged in the incident system.
  2nd violation (within 30 days): Written warning. Mandatory 15-minute
     helmet safety briefing. Logged with supervisor signature.
  3rd violation (within 30 days): Removal from zone pending disciplinary
     review. EHS manager notified. Retraining required before return.

==============================================================================
SECTION 9 — EMERGENCY RESPONSE
==============================================================================
If a worker sustains a head injury:
  1. Activate the emergency response procedure immediately.
  2. Do not remove the helmet if the worker is unconscious or the injury
     appears to involve the neck or spine.
  3. Contact emergency services.
  4. Preserve the incident scene for investigation.
  5. Retain the helmet involved in the incident for analysis.
  6. Complete a full incident report within 24 hours.
