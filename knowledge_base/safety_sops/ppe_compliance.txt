DEMONSTRATION SAFETY GUIDANCE — SmartFactory Copilot
Category: PPE Compliance
Document: ppe_compliance.txt
NOTE: This document is bundled as demonstration guidance for the SmartFactory AI Copilot
project. It is NOT an official industrial regulation or certified safety standard.
Consult your organisation's EHS department and applicable national regulations for
legally binding requirements.

==============================================================================
SECTION 1 — PURPOSE
==============================================================================
This guidance document defines the minimum Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
requirements for workers operating inside SmartFactory facilities. It establishes
the roles and responsibilities of supervisors, safety officers, and individual
workers in ensuring PPE compliance at all times.

==============================================================================
SECTION 2 — SCOPE
==============================================================================
This guidance applies to:
- All production floor workers.
- Maintenance and engineering staff.
- Visitors and contractors entering designated PPE zones.
- All factory zones including Assembly Areas, Loading Docks, Warehouses, and
  Packing Areas.

==============================================================================
SECTION 3 — MANDATORY PPE REQUIREMENTS
==============================================================================
3.1 Hard Hat / Safety Helmet
  - Mandatory in all areas where overhead work, forklift operations, or
    material storage is active.
  - Class E helmets required in areas with electrical hazards.
  - Must be inspected before each shift for cracks, dents, or damage.
  - Replacement interval: every 5 years, or immediately after any significant
    impact event.

3.2 High-Visibility Safety Vest
  - Mandatory in all areas where forklifts, automated guided vehicles (AGVs),
    or heavy machinery operate.
  - Minimum Class 2 reflective vest required on production floors.
  - Must remain fastened at all times — open or partially worn vests are
    considered non-compliant.

3.3 Safety Mask / Respiratory Protection
  - Required in areas with dust, chemical exposure, or airborne particulates
    above permissible exposure limits (PEL).
  - Minimum FFP2 / N95 rated mask in designated respiratory hazard zones.
  - Disposable masks must not be reused across shifts.

3.4 Safety Goggles / Eye Protection
  - Required in welding areas, chemical handling zones, and areas with
    projectile or splash risk.
  - Must meet ANSI Z87.1 or equivalent national standard.

==============================================================================
SECTION 4 — SUPERVISOR RESPONSIBILITIES
==============================================================================
- Conduct a visual PPE compliance check at the start of each shift.
- Remove any worker from the production floor who is non-compliant until
  appropriate PPE is obtained.
- Document all PPE violations using the facility's incident logging system.
- Escalate repeat violations (3 or more within 30 days) to the EHS manager.
- Ensure replacement PPE stock is available at all entry checkpoints.

==============================================================================
SECTION 5 — CORRECTIVE ACTIONS FOR VIOLATIONS
==============================================================================
First violation:
  - Verbal warning.
  - Immediate correction required before resuming work.
  - Entry in incident log.

Second violation (same worker, within 30 days):
  - Written warning issued.
  - Mandatory PPE refresher training (1 hour).
  - Supervisor notified.

Third violation (same worker, within 30 days):
  - Removal from production floor for the shift.
  - Disciplinary review meeting.
  - Review of work assignment suitability.

==============================================================================
SECTION 6 — INCIDENT RESPONSE PROCEDURE
==============================================================================
When a PPE violation is detected by the CCTV monitoring system or by supervisors:
  1. Alert is raised in the incident management system.
  2. The supervising operator acknowledges the alert within 5 minutes.
  3. The worker is approached and guided to the nearest PPE station.
  4. The incident is logged with: worker ID, zone, violation type, timestamp,
     and corrective action taken.
  5. If the worker refuses to comply, the area supervisor is notified immediately.

==============================================================================
SECTION 7 — PPE INSPECTION AND MAINTENANCE
==============================================================================
- PPE stations must be stocked and checked at the beginning of every shift.
- Damaged or defective PPE must be removed from circulation immediately.
- Helmets and vests with visible damage (tears, cracks, discolouration) must
  not be returned to stock.
- Maintenance records for reusable PPE (respirators, goggles) must be kept
  for a minimum of 2 years.

==============================================================================
SECTION 8 — TRAINING REQUIREMENTS
==============================================================================
- All new workers must complete a PPE induction before entering any production
  zone (minimum 30 minutes).
- Annual PPE refresher training is mandatory for all production staff.
- Training records must be retained for 3 years.
